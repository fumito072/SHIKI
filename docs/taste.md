# Taste — the user's preferences across works

Agents read this before making or changing any work, and append one dated bullet when a piece of feedback states a
general preference (not one specific to a single work). Keep bullets short and concrete. Work-specific history goes in
`works/<id>/NOTES.md`.

## Aim (from docs/philosophy.md)

- Widen the gap between what the brain predicts and what it sees. Break the boundaries of our world.

## Likes

- 2026-10-03 · A strong still image with a literary atmosphere (MOONSEA's moon on the water recalls the cover of
  *The Moon Is a Harsh Mistress*). Stillness and composition matter as much as motion.
- 2026-10-03 · Ink should behave like a liquid: slimy, stretching, folding into curves.
- 2026-10-03 · Cyber/digital texture blended with natural phenomena (UI direction, also a hint for works).

## Dislikes

- 2026-10-03 · Visuals that merely vibrate or pulse with the audio. Sound must drive choreography (anticipation,
  tension → release, inertia, broken expectations), not a level meter.
- 2026-10-03 · Elements whose purpose cannot be read (MOONSEA v1's particle line: "what is it trying to do?").
- 2026-10-03 · Prompt-only, "AI-looking", tacky imagery.
